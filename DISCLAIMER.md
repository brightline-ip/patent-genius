## Additional notes for Patent Genius

- **Structural checks only.** Patent Genius never judges legal sufficiency, never decides claim scope, and uses no
  model for interpretation. Office-action parsing is rule-based and has been validated only on section headings;
  every finding is unverified until a practitioner checks it against the office action itself.
- **Generated drafts.** Every generated document or report carries the header "DRAFT — generated by Patent Genius;
  not legal advice; requires review by a registered practitioner". Do not remove it from material that has not been
  reviewed.
- **Statistics are descriptive.** Rejection rates and similar figures come only from data you build or download
  yourself, show correlations, and are not predictions for your matter.
